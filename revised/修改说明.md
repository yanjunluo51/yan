### 0.2 本轮新写的关键文字

**摘要（250 词）**
> Stage-gate committees decide the timing of irreversible investments under regulatory ambiguity, and members weighting adverse regulatory evidence differently can reach different timing decisions at the same price. Real-options analysis reduces this decision to one project-level trigger, and committee decision support compresses member assessments into a score or ranking, so neither reports the price range over which member classifications differ. To close this gap, this study derives one price threshold per member from affine combinations of two endpoint triggers, so a committee can locate disagreement on the price axis before any vote. ... Monte Carlo committee panels then measured the investment-timing information lost by compressing member thresholds into one trigger. ... One classification thus suffices only when the prevailing price lies outside the contested interval.

- 摘要的核心数字仍只有 2.74%、10%、88.5%、15 of 32 四个。
- 末句换成一个新的总结性判断：价格落在争议区间外时，一个分类就够了。原末句和第三句意思重复，所以换掉。

**引言贡献段**
> The paper makes one main contribution and two supporting contributions. The main contribution is a member-indexed gate report. ... The supporting contributions fall into two groups. The first group is analytical and yields a location rule for the critical weight. ... The second group is methodological and yields a release rule for the report. ...

- 主贡献：成员阈值向量和争议价格区间组成的报告，用来确定当前价格在每位成员的评估下是否得到同样的分类。
- 解析类贡献得到临界权重的位置规则：唯一性来自仿射规则的严格单调性，曲率决定它在 1/2 哪一侧，且这一侧随坐标（标准差或方差）变化。
- 方法类贡献得到发布规则：用基准比较和分类保真度分析测量精度，再用有限登记表把精度限制转换成单独发布仿射报告的条件。
- 案例的两个主要结果放在贡献段之后，单独成段。

**文献综述的三个 gap**
- 2.1 末："Across this stream, the reported object is a project-level trigger, a value, a policy, or group behavior after a vote. The first gap is therefore the absence of a threshold for each member before the vote. ..."
- 2.2 末："No reviewed method converts recorded member weights into thresholds on the price axis, and this missing report is the second gap."
- 2.3 末："None of them supplied an input to a member-level screening rule, which is the third gap."
- 末段："Table 1 summarizes the three gaps. First, ... Second, ... Third, ..."，接着说明本文如何回应前两个 gap，第三个 gap 如何作为可替换的输入处理。

**结论**
- 过渡句加重点句：
  > The analytical results, the benchmark evaluation, and the CCUS reconstruction jointly support one central finding. Within the maintained model, the member-threshold vector and the contested-price interval showed at each gate whether all members classified the prevailing price alike.
- Limitations 段：
  - (A) 最重要的局限："The most important limitation is the maintained price model."
  - (i) 是什么：所有成员阈值都在永续几何布朗运动、只有波动率存在模糊性的假设下推导。
  - (ii) 为什么重要：阈值水平和区间边界取决于价格过程；在均值回归下，锚定阈值的移动超过了参考边距。
  - (iii) 为什么在本文中仍可接受：存档价格没有拒绝模型假设的持续性；稳健性网格中，均值回归、有限期限和跳跃下临界权重都仍在 1/2 以上；预期价格过程不同的评审关口要先重新校准，登记表也不允许未登记配置单独发布仿射报告。
- 最后一段：
  > The proposed report therefore moves the detection of member disagreement to an earlier stage of the stage-gate process. By using one recorded weight per member and the prevailing price, it shows whether the committee agrees before any vote is taken. Where the members disagree, the report locates the disagreement in price units and states whether the affine rule can stand alone.
