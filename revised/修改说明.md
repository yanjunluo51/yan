- **句子长度**：若把引文和数学式计入字数，仍有十余句达到 25 词以上。
- **时态**：约 20 处本文的数值结果仍用现在时，例如：
  - 表 3 注中的 "binds"
  - "This value is about 2.9 times ..."

  - "The recomputation kept the gate state" 与后两句同义。
- **绝对化表述**："None of these project-level formulations returns ..."、"At committee scale, this cost is negligible."、"The recorded weights have no substitute ..."。
